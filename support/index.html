Common questions

Where are my thoughts stored? On your iPhone only. Aside does not send them anywhere, and there is no account.

Can I move my thoughts to a new iPhone? Use Export all thoughts in Settings, or restore an iPhone backup made with iCloud or your computer.

How do I get my thoughts out? Settings, then Export all thoughts.

How do I delete everything? Settings, then Delete everything.

I have Aside Plus on another iPhone. Open Settings in Aside and choose Get Aside Plus, then Restore purchase.

How do I cancel Aside Plus? In your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Aside. Aside itself stays free, and your thoughts stay yours.

Var kan jag återställa eller avsluta Plus? Återställ under Inställningar i Aside. Avsluta i iPhonens Inställningar under ditt namn och Prenumerationer.
