Common questions

Where are my thoughts stored? On your iPhone. If iCloud backup is on, a copy is kept in your own private iCloud.

How do I get my thoughts out? Settings, then Export all thoughts.

How do I delete everything? Settings, then Delete everything.

I bought Aside on another iPhone. Open the offer and choose Restore purchase.
